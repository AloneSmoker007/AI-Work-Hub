# Offer & Price Hypothesis — Astrologers (DRAFT / UNVALIDATED)

> **Status: working hypothesis.** Nothing on this page is a validated market
> fact. Every number and assumption below must be tested through real
> conversations with astrologers before it is quoted as firm pricing.
> See `outreach-list.md` for the validation plan.

## Chosen niche

**Astrologers** (WhatsApp-first astrology consultants — birth chart,
compatibility, and life-path readings).

Rationale (hypothesis): astrology consultations are already sold and scheduled
through WhatsApp/Instagram DMs in many markets; pricing questions ("how much
for a birth chart?") are repetitive; a quote/booking landing page maps
naturally onto the QuickQuote template architecture.

## What we sell

1. **WhatsApp-first astrology quote/booking landing page** — visitors pick a
   reading, see a starting estimate, and send their details to the
   astrologer's WhatsApp in one tap.
2. **Customized branding, services and WhatsApp CTA** — the astrologer's name,
   logo/colors, reading list, prices, and WhatsApp number.
3. **One-time setup/customization** — configuration, copy edits, deployment,
   and the verified QA pass.
4. **Optional maintenance** — ongoing price/copy updates and small changes.

## Price hypothesis (NOT validated)

| Item | Hypothesis range | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| One-time setup & customization | **$150–$300** per site | Low — untested |
| Optional maintenance | **$20–$40 / month** | Low — untested |
| Extra revision beyond included round | $25–$50 each | Low — untested |

Anchor notes (hypothesis only):

- Price against the astrologer's income per reading, not against "a website
  costs $X". If one reading earns $49+, the page pays for itself with a
  handful of extra bookings — that is the pitch, not a promise.
- Do **not** display these prices on the customer-facing demo site. The demo
  site shows reading prices only (demo values, see `CUSTOMIZE.md`).
- Discounting: prefer including one revision round over lowering the price.

## Open questions to validate (in order)

1. How do astrologers currently quote and book? (DMs, phone, existing site?)
2. What does a typical reading cost in the target market? (sanity-check the
   demo's $49 / $89 / $149 sample prices)
3. Does "starting estimate → WhatsApp" match how they price readings, or do
   they quote per question?
4. Will they pay one-time, monthly, or only per lead?
5. Which languages/scripts must the page support?
